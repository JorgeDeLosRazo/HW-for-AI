Power Estimation Report — crossbar_top (Memristor Crossbar Analog AI Accelerator)
Tool: Manual estimation from yosys cell counts + sky130_fd_sc_hd characterization
Note: OpenLane power estimation (via OpenSTA/PrimeTime-PX equivalent) requires
      a complete synthesis run with PDK mapping. OpenLane failed at the
      Verilator.Lint step (verilator not installed) so no automated power
      report is available. Estimates below follow the standard switching-power
      model: P = α × C_eff × V_dd² × f

=== power model inputs ===

   VDD                     : 1.8 V  (sky130A nominal)
   Clock frequency         : 100 MHz
   Cell count (estimated)  : ~4000 sky130 std cells after PDK mapping
   Activity factor (α)     : 0.2   (typical for MAC datapath, not streaming)
   Average C_eff/cell      : 2.5 fF  (sky130_fd_sc_hd medium drive strength)

=== dynamic power estimate ===

   P_dynamic = α × N_cells × C_eff × V_dd² × f
             = 0.2 × 4000 × 2.5e-15 × (1.8)² × 100e6
             ≈ 0.648 mW

=== static (leakage) power estimate ===

   Leakage per cell (sky130_fd_sc_hd tt/25°C): ~0.5 nW/cell
   P_leakage = 4000 × 0.5e-9 = 2.0 µW  (negligible)

=== total estimated power ===

   P_total ≈ 0.65 mW  (dominated by dynamic switching)

=== energy per MVM operation ===

   MVM latency: 4 voltage cycles × 10 ns/cycle + overhead ≈ 60 ns/MVM
   (includes weight load time amortized over 100 MVMs per weight set)
   E_MVM = P_total × t_MVM = 0.65e-3 × 60e-9 ≈ 39 pJ per 4×4 MVM

=== comparison to M1 CPU baseline ===

   CPU (Intel i5-8350U, addmm kernel): ~228 ms / inference
   CPU TDP: ~15 W → energy/inference ≈ 228e-3 × 15 = 3.42 J
   Accelerator energy/MVM ≈ 39 pJ → for equivalent 128×768 GEMM:
     N_tiles = (128 × 768) / 16 = 6144 tiles
     E_accelerator ≈ 6144 × 39e-12 ≈ 239 nJ   (≈ 14 million× lower energy)
